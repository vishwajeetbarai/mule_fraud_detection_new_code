## 1. Research Problem and Motivation

Digital bank account opening fraud and mule account acquisition represent severe threats to the global financial architecture. Criminal networks leverage synthetic identities, compromised personal information, and automated application scripts to open high volumes of transactional accounts. Once established, these accounts serve as "mules" to launder the proceeds of cybercrime, ransomware, and human trafficking before being abandoned or frozen.

### The Research Defensibility Gap:
Conventional academic machine learning papers on fraud detection frequently suffer from severe methodological weaknesses that undermine their scientific and operational validity:
1. **Unrealistic Data Partitioning**: Applying randomized K-fold cross-validation across time series data, causing severe future-to-past temporal lookahead leakage.
2. **Test-Label Contamination in Decision Tuning**: Using final out-of-sample test labels to tune cost-minimizing decision thresholds or ROC operating points.
3. **Ignoring Concept Drift**: Treating applicant distributions as stationary, failing to monitor feature and score shifts over chronological horizons.
4. **Invalid Normal Uncertainty Approximations**: Utilizing asymptotic Wald confidence intervals ($\hat{p} \pm 1.96 \text{SE}$) on extreme class-imbalanced data ($p \approx 0.01$), resulting in negative probability bounds.
5. **Instantaneous Feedback Assumption**: Assuming ground-truth fraud labels become known immediately upon transaction scoring, ignoring the 30–90 day verification latency inherent to financial dispute resolution.
6. **Synthetic Adversarial Assumptions**: Applying arbitrary $L_p$ gradient noise directly to post-processed feature embeddings without respecting domain constraints or recalculating derived graph and velocity features.

This project resolves these critical deficiencies by designing, implementing, and validating an end-to-end research framework that integrates **cost-sensitive optimization, prequential drift-aware adaptation (TDTA), split conformal prediction uncertainty sets, and a three-tier hierarchical triage policy** across the complete 1,000,000-row NeurIPS 2022 Bank Account Fraud benchmark.

## 2. Dataset Description, Limitations & Framing

### Dataset Specification:
The research evaluates the **NeurIPS 2022 Bank Account Fraud (BAF) suite (`BaseDataset.csv`)**:
- **Total Records**: Exactly **1,000,000 applications**.
- **Temporal Duration**: 8 discrete chronological months ($m \in \{0, 1, \dots, 7\}$).
- **Target Variable**: `fraud_bool` $\in \{0, 1\}$, where 1 indicates an illegitimate/fraudulent application.
- **Overall Class Imbalance**: 11,029 frauds (**1.1029%**) vs 988,971 legitimate instances (**98.8971%**).

### Academic Scoping & Honest Framing:
It is scientifically essential to state clearly what the BAF dataset represents:
- **Synthetic/Simulated Benchmark**: The BAF dataset was generated by researchers using CTGAN trained on real-world bank application and credit bureau data to preserve differential privacy and commercial confidentiality.
- **Bank-Account Opening Fraud vs Confirmed Mule Activity**: The ground-truth labels identify fraudulent account-opening attempts (synthetic identities, stolen credentials, and application manipulation). While mule rings predominantly operate by opening such fraudulent accounts, the dataset does not track post-opening transaction graphs or confirmed anti-money laundering (AML) laundering cycles.
- **Defensible Framing**: The implemented task is **bank-account opening fraud detection**, serving as the mandatory initial gatekeeper against mule account acquisition. We do not claim to have detected confirmed money mules inside live banking ledgers.

## 3. Data Preprocessing & Feature Engineering

Preprocessing and feature generation are strictly isolated inside `FeaturePreprocessor`:
1. **Fit Isolation**: Category vocabularies and rolling baseline statistics ($mean, std$) are learned strictly on the historical training partition (Months 0–4). Months 5, 6, and 7 are transformed strictly out-of-sample.
2. **Domain Sentinel Missingness**: Explicit boolean indicators (`_missing`) are extracted for all sentinel columns (`prev_address_months_count`, `current_address_months_count`, `bank_months_count`, `session_length_in_minutes`, `device_distinct_emails_8w`, `intended_balcon_amount`) before standard imputation.
3. **Domain-Engineered Representations (86 Features Total)**:
   - **Velocity Ratios & Acceleration**: Short-term burst velocity relative to background history (`velocity_ratio_6h_24h`, `velocity_surge_intensity`, `velocity_burst_acceleration`, `velocity_spike_index`).
   - **Behavioral Graph Centrality Proxies**: Bipartite projection proxies tracking device-email collisions and DoB-email collisions across the banking network (`device_sharing_density`, `device_recycling_speed`, `graph_hub_degree_proxy`, `ring_clique_density_proxy`).
   - **Financial Exposure & Incoherence**: Credit limit strain relative to income (`credit_limit_to_income`) and identity-channel incoherence (`name_email_free_mismatch`).
   - **Rolling Drift Z-Scores**: Historical normalized baseline adjustments (`velocity_6h_drift_zscore`, `credit_risk_score_drift_zscore`).

## 6. The Proposed Hierarchical Triage Framework

To bridge the gap between binary classification scores and operational bank decision-making, the framework implements a **three-tier hierarchical decision policy**:

```
                               Incoming Application p_i
                                          |
                +-------------------------+-------------------------+
                |                                                   |
     Is Gamma_{alpha} == {0}                             Is p_i >= max(tau_cost, tau_tdta)
     AND p_i < min(tau_cost, tau_tdta)?                  OR Gamma_{alpha} == {1}?
                |                                                   |
               YES                                                 YES
                |                                                   |
                v                                                   v
        [ AUTO_APPROVE ]                                      [ AUTO_FLAG ]
     (Frictionless Pass)                                    (Direct Intercept)
                |                                                   |
                NO                                                 NO
                +-------------------------+-------------------------+
                                          |
                                          v
                                  [ HUMAN_REVIEW ]
                           (Manual Investigator Queue)
                     - Ambiguous Conformal Sets: {0, 1}
                     - Policy Conflict: tau_min <= p_i < tau_max
```

### Full-Scale Triage Operational Distribution ($N=205,011$):
- **`AUTO_APPROVE` (Action 0)**: **113,493 applications (55.36%)**
  - Legitimate passed frictionlessly: 113,321 (99.85% clean).
  - Frauds erroneously approved (escape): 172 (0.15% leakage).
- **`HUMAN_REVIEW` (Action 2)**: **78,796 applications (38.44%)**
  - Legitimate referred for inspection: 77,668.
  - Frauds caught in inspection: **1,128**.
- **`AUTO_FLAG` (Action 1)**: **12,722 applications (6.21%)**
  - Legitimate false alarms: 11,144.
  - Frauds directly auto-flagged: **1,578**.

### The Conformal Safety-Net Realized:
- **Direct Automated Detection Rate**: $1,578 / 2,878 =$ **54.83%**.
- **Overall Hierarchical Triage Recall**: $(1,578 + 1,128) / 2,878 =$ **94.02% [0.9310, 0.9483]**.
- **Fraud Escape Rate**: Only $172 / 2,878 =$ **5.98%**.
- *Core Finding*: While a standalone threshold catches only 54.8% of frauds automatically, the conformal safety-net catches an additional 1,128 frauds in the uncertain review queue, achieving **94.02% total fraud interception**.

## 14. Key Research Contributions

1. **Elimination of Data Leakage**: Enforced zero test-label leakage across all threshold selection, calibration, and policy tuning paths.
2. **Prequential Realism**: Implemented and validated prequential delayed-label verification up to 60 days, establishing that triage safety is preserved under realistic operational lag.
3. **Conformal Defense-in-Depth**: Proved that split conformal prediction sets act as an operational safety net, recovering over 1,100 fraudulent applications missed by standalone decision thresholds.
4. **Domain-Constrained Adversarial Evaluation**: Constructed a realistic threat framework perturbing raw input data prior to feature engineering, preserving derived behavioral graph invariants.
5. **Non-Asymptotic Uncertainty Quantification**: Replaced invalid Wald approximations with exact Wilson Score and stratified bootstrap intervals, providing publication-defensible confidence bounds.
